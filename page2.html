Why I Love Music

Favorite Genres

2023 was the year I discovered genres so new to me. My friend brought me to a local punk show, I was nervous, too scared I wasn't going to fit in. Once the loud instrumentals and screaming vocals started playing, I was starstruck. Seeing how people started crowd surfing and pushing each other in the pit, it was awesome! Screamo became my favorite genre so quickly. I then learned about folk, emo, and so much more. I love learning about new sub-genres and finding artists within those genres.

How I Find New Music

What I like to do whenever I find out a new genre, I go on spotify and look up playlists that fall under the genre. I listen to some of the music, and whatever I enjoyed, I look into the bands a bit more. I also go on social media, watching videos talking about new or unknown bands.

How I rate albums

I don't "rate" albums, I can't bring myself to rate the songs out of 10. Espically when the album was amazing, or if there's too many songs to rate. I can tell I enjoyed it, if it comes back into my life again. For example, a song called "Spirit Desire" by Tigers Jaw I discovered back in sophmore year of highschool. This song has came in and out of my life so many times. It's not on any of my playlists, I just think of it and decide to lisen to it again.
