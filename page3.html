Recently Discovered Albums

Apiary

Gingerbee

I got a tiktok talking about this album that recently came out, so I decided to listen to it. I absolutley fell in love with it from beginning to end. It's a mix of screamo and jazz, such two different genres that blend well together. Even though it's an EP with only 6 songs, it's such a short and wonderful listen everytime. After just one listen, I bought their cds for both of their EPs.

Favorite song: "Feeling Like Children/Honey"

Forward

First Dack Back

I was on spotify and scrolling through my discover weekly playlist, which is a playlist that shows you shows you songs that you might enjoy. As I listened to "Sure, Ok", I fell in love with the album. I had it on repeat for weeks, it helped me get through the summer of last year. I bought a cd as soon as they went on sale on their bandcamp.

Favorite song: "12 Mile Train Tracks"

Rosemary

Porcelain Stars

One of the band members for Gingerbee was promoting Porcelain Stars on his instagram. I listened to it while getting ready for bed, and it was amazing. The violin in the album was beautiful, the vocals were amazing as well. This album got me intrested in listening to more jazz.

Favorite song: "Endless,Dreamless"

It Always Starts mid Winter

Arranged in April

This band was coming to ogden, I've seen videos online, but I've barely listened to their music before. I decided to buy a ticket and go, since I wanted to do something fun before finals. It was the best decision ever, their music live was amazing. I bought all three cds at the show, and got them all signed by the members!

Favorite song: "Oil & Water/Bare Bones"
